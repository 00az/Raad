### Summary

The functional design is substantively complete and internally coherent for the walking skeleton's scope. The two Major findings are advisory: the WF5 boundary ambiguity and the AC3.2.1 traceability mismatch do not introduce architectural unsoundness, but they create real implementation risk — a developer tracing WF5 could implement the recipient-resolution call and the confirmation POST as a single server-side sequence, and the missing projected-balance rule could lead to that display obligation being omitted from code review. The three Minor findings are small but each touches the C1 contract; the email omission in BR5.2 is the most consequential of the three. All five findings are addressable with targeted edits before code generation begins.
