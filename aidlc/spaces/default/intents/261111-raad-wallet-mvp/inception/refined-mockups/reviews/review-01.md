### Summary

Coverage is complete. Every Must Have and Should Have story with a screen maps to S1 to S12, and US6.1, US6.2 and US6.4 have no screen. The limits (1.00 to 500.00 SAR, whole halalas), the identical sign-in refusal, the review and confirm step, the code entry reachable from sign-in, the "could not confirm" result, the offline rules and the loading, empty and error states all agree across the four documents. Answers Q2 to Q9 are honoured.

The contrast figures are plausible. The checklist is concrete, and it correctly declines to claim full WCAG conformance. The environment chip is adequately flagged as outside the stories, but it is drawn on S1 and S11 and listed in the global rules as if settled. Treat it as an optional addition to approve or drop at the gate. The two Major items are decisions or corrections for the gate (the expired-code dead end and the logo provenance), not blockers to the design as a whole.

Optional suggestions, not grounds for NOT-READY:
- Note that 44 dp touch targets exceed the WCAG 2.1 AA minimum, so they are a design choice and not an AA requirement.
- Give the disabled Continue and Confirm buttons a visible reason on every screen where they can turn off.
